### Hi, I'm Mathieu 👋

I turn data and AI into tools that people actually use, from deal analysis to day-to-day automation.

I work on **data, AI and automation for private equity** at **Adagia Partners**: LLM copilots for due diligence, data pipelines and dashboards for portfolio companies, and the workflows that link them together. Engineering background from **CentraleSupélec**.

#### 🔭 What I build

- **LLM applications**: RAG, document and spreadsheet analysis, voice interfaces, agentic workflows
- **Data products**: pipelines, BI dashboards, ML models from exploration to production
- **Automation**: workflows that remove the repetitive part of the job
